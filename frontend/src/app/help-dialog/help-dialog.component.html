Employee Management Help

Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a summary of employee statistics, including total employees, recent hires, and department distributions. Use the quick links to navigate to key sections.

Managing Employees

- To add a new employee, click the "Add Employee" button and fill out the required details such as name, designation, department, and contact information.
- To update an employee's information, locate the employee in the list and click the "Edit" icon.
- To remove an employee, click the "Delete" icon next to their name. Confirm the action in the pop-up.

Leave Management

The leave management section allows you to track employee leave requests and approve or reject them as needed. Use the "Leaves" tab to access pending and historical leave data.

Reports

Generate detailed reports on employee attendance, performance, and departmental metrics. Navigate to the "Reports" section, select the report type, and specify a date range.
